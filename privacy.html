By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. 1. Information We Collect

We collect information about you in connection with your use of the Services. The types of information we collect, and the means by which we collect it, are described below.

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

We collect information that you provide directly when you register for an account, complete your profile, or otherwise interact with the Services. This information may include:

  • Account information, including your email address and password, the languages you speak, and the languages you wish to learn;
  • Profile information, including your name, date of birth, gender, country of residence, profile photograph, background image, gallery images, biographical description, interests, and topics you select;
  • Content and contributions, including posts, comments, replies, reactions, events, event participation, and any other materials you create or submit through the Services;
  • Communications, including direct messages exchanged with other users and any communications you send to us, such as customer support enquiries or feedback;
  • Reports and complaints, including the content of any reports you submit regarding other users or content within the Services.

1.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you access or use the Services, we may automatically collect certain information about your interaction with the Services and the device you use to access them. This information may include:

  • Usage information, including actions you take within the Services, the content you view or interact with, the features you use, the frequency and duration of your sessions, and other engagement metrics;
  • Device information, including device model, operating system and version, mobile network information, application version, device identifiers, screen resolution, and language and time zone settings;
  • Log and diagnostic information, including application logs, crash reports, performance data, and error reports;
  • Approximate location information, which may be derived from information you provide (such as the location associated with an event you create or attend) or, with your permission, from your device;
  • Advertising identifiers, including Google Advertising ID and similar identifiers used by our advertising partners to deliver and measure advertising, subject to your device settings and the controls described in Section 11 (Advertising).

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third-party sources, including:

  • Authentication providers, if you elect to register or sign in to the Services using a third-party authentication service. Such providers may share your name, email address, profile photograph, and other identifying information in accordance with their settings and policies;
  • Other users, who may provide information about you in connection with reports, content they submit, or communications they send through the Services.

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, maintain, personalize, and improve the Services and their features;
  • To create, administer, and authenticate your account, and to verify your identity in connection with use of the Services;
  • To facilitate language exchange, communication, and other interactions between users of the Services;
  • To deliver notifications, including in-app notifications, push notifications, and email communications relating to your account, your activity, or the Services;
  • To respond to your enquiries, provide customer support, and address technical issues you may experience;
  • To monitor, detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent, abusive, harmful, or otherwise unauthorized activity, and to enforce our Terms of Service and other policies;
  • To display advertising within the Services and to measure the performance of that advertising;
  • To analyze and understand how the Services are used, including to measure the effectiveness of features and to inform product development;
  • To conduct research and develop new features and services;
  • To comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements;
  • For any other purpose with your consent.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information about you in the limited circumstances described below.

Information that you elect to make publicly available through the Services, including your profile information, posts, comments, events, and other contributions, will be visible to other users of the Services. Direct messages and other private communications are visible only to the users you send them to. You should consider carefully what information you choose to share publicly.

We engage third-party service providers to perform functions on our behalf in connection with the Services. These functions include, without limitation, hosting and infrastructure, authentication, database management, file and image storage, push notification delivery, analytics, crash and diagnostic reporting, translation, and customer support. We provide these service providers with only the information they need to perform their functions, and we require them to handle that information in accordance with appropriate confidentiality and security obligations.

3.3 For Legal, Regulatory, and Safety Reasons

We may access, preserve, or disclose information about you where we believe in good faith that doing so is necessary or appropriate to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; to enforce our Terms of Service and other agreements; to detect, prevent, or otherwise address fraud, security, or technical issues; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Lngual, our users, or the public.

3.4 In Connection with a Corporate Transaction

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, restructuring, financing, sale of assets, or similar corporate transaction, or in the unlikely event of bankruptcy or insolvency, your information may be transferred or disclosed in connection with that transaction, subject to customary confidentiality protections.

3.5 With Your Consent or at Your Direction

We may share your information for any other purpose disclosed to you and with your consent, or at your direction.

3.6 With Advertising Partners

We share limited information, including advertising identifiers, device information, and general usage information, with third-party advertising partners for the purpose of delivering and measuring advertisements within the Services. Further information regarding advertising and the choices available to you is provided in Section 11 (Advertising).

3.7 Aggregated and De-Identified Information

11. Advertising

The Services include advertising, including advertising provided by third-party advertising networks and partners. These partners receive limited information, including advertising identifiers, device information, and general usage information, in order to deliver advertisements and to measure their performance. The advertising you see is based on general information such as the country in which you use the Services, rather than on a detailed individual profile.

If you reside in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction where consent is required, the Services will request your consent to the use of advertising identifiers and related processing the first time you open the application, in accordance with applicable data protection law. You may withdraw or change your consent at any time from in-app Settings.

You may also control the use of advertising identifiers and the personalization of advertising through your device settings:

  • Android: open Settings, then Privacy, then Ads, and select the option to delete or reset your advertising ID, or to opt out of personalized advertising;
  • iOS: open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking and Apple Advertising, and adjust the relevant options.

Disabling personalized advertising does not remove advertising from the Services, but it limits the use of your information to personalize that advertising. Subscribing to Lngual Plus removes advertising from the Services entirely.
